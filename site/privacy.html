FuelBuddy privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

FuelBuddy is a fuel log app for Android, published by PUBLISHER_NAME. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your fuel records stay on your phone.

Your data stays on your phone

Vehicles, fill-ups, running costs, reminders, logbook trips and settings are stored only in a database on your device. FuelBuddy has no account, no server and no cloud sync, and it never uploads your fuel records. We cannot see your data.

Route planner (online)

The route planner is the only part of FuelBuddy that sends trip information off your phone. When you use it, the place names you search for and the start and destination you choose are sent to OpenStreetMap community services (Photon by Komoot for search, the FOSSGIS routing server for directions), and map images are loaded from a map tile service. If you tap "Use my location", your approximate position is used once to fill in the start point; FuelBuddy does not track your location. "Navigate in Google Maps" hands the route to Google Maps, under Google's privacy policy.

Scanning fuel slips

When you scan a fuel slip, you take a photo with Android's camera app or pick one with Android's photo picker. The text is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit. The photo is not uploaded and is not kept by FuelBuddy. FuelBuddy does not have camera or photo-library permission.

Fuel prices

Official fuel prices are built into the app. FuelBuddy also downloads a small file of newer prices from Google Drive. That request contains no information about you or your vehicles.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, and your approximate location from your IP address to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. Ad providers never receive your fuel, vehicle or trip data. FuelBuddy Pro removes ads completely.

Purchases

FuelBuddy Pro is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; FuelBuddy only learns whether Pro has been purchased.

No tracking

FuelBuddy contains no analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting services, and does not sell or share personal information.

Exports and backups

When you export to CSV, export your travel logbook or back up your data, the file is created on your phone and handed to the app you choose (for example Google Drive or email). Where it goes from there is up to you. Separately, Android may include app data in your personal Google device backup if you have turned that on in your phone's settings.

Children

FuelBuddy is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all data removes all vehicles, fill-ups, running costs, reminders and trips. Uninstalling FuelBuddy deletes its database. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Permissions

Internet access (ads, Google Play purchases, fuel prices, route planner); location, only when you tap "Use my location"; notifications, for reminders and fuel price alerts.
