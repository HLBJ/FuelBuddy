## Full description (4000)

```
FuelBuddy is the fuel log made for South African drivers. Record a fill-up in seconds, or just photograph the slip, and see exactly what your car uses and costs.

⛽ SCAN YOUR FUEL SLIP
Point your camera at the till slip and FuelBuddy fills in the litres, price, date and garage for you. The slip is read on your phone, even without signal.

📈 OFFICIAL PETROL AND DIESEL PRICES
FuelBuddy knows the official monthly prices for 93 and 95 petrol and 50ppm and 500ppm diesel, inland and coastal. The petrol price fills itself in, and when the new month's prices are announced you'll see what a full tank will cost, plus a reminder the evening before the change so you can fill up before an increase.

🛢️ KNOW WHEN TO FILL UP
See how much fuel is left, how far you can still drive and the day you'll need to fill up, worked out from your own driving.

📒 SARS TRAVEL LOGBOOK
Log business and private trips per tax year with your odometer readings. Use "log the return trip" to do two trips at once. At tax time, export a logbook with the details SARS asks for, as a PDF or spreadsheet (Pro).

💰 THE TRUE COST OF DRIVING
Add services, tyres, insurance, licence fees, tolls and parking, and see your all-in cost per kilometre and per month, and where the money goes (Pro).

🔔 NEVER MISS A LICENCE DISC RENEWAL
Reminders for your licence disc, services and tyres, by date or odometer. Licence disc reminders roll over to next year by themselves.

🗺️ PLAN A ROAD TRIP
Plan a route, choose how many fuel stops you want and see the best stations and the toll plazas on the way. Then navigate in Google Maps.

📊 CLEAR STATISTICS
Fuel consumption (L/100 km) measured the accurate way, from full tank to full tank. Cost per km, monthly spending, your best and worst tanks, and how your vehicles compare.

🔄 SWITCH IN ONE TAP
Coming from Fuelio, Drivvo, Fuelly or a spreadsheet? Import your history and your statistics are ready straight away.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up and no tracking. Your fuel data stays on your phone. Back it up to Google Drive or email whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone.

ALSO
• Unlimited vehicles: petrol, diesel, hybrid or LPG
• Home-screen widget and shortcuts for quick fill-ups
• Trip cost calculator
• Export everything to CSV
• Light and dark mode
• Rands, kilometres and litres throughout

FUELBUDDY PRO
One payment, no subscription. Pro removes the ads and adds advanced statistics, the true cost of driving and SARS logbook export. Try every Pro feature free for 7 days first.

Fuel prices are the official prices published by the Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources (Gauteng for inland, the coast for coastal); prices in your town may differ by a few cents. FuelBuddy is not affiliated with SARS. Keep your logbook and tax records according to SARS's requirements.
```
